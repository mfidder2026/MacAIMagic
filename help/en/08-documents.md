Make Word documents without cloud AI. A **project** is a folder with:
- **attachments**: context and information, such as requirements, examples and notes;
- a **house style**: only the layout (a .docx);
- the **documents** you make.

## How it works
1. Make a project and add attachments and a house style.
2. Ask, for example: "make an AQS". The model looks in the attachments for what the document must contain, and searches the web for what they do not say.
3. You get a **Markdown draft** with numbered sources. You change all text there.
4. Last, the app makes the **.docx** in your house style: cover, headers and footers, numbered headings, tables and table of contents.

You fill in what the cover needs once per project.

> A fast model does the small work (notes per page, spelling) and a heavy model the writing. Choose them under Models with **Fast** and **Heavy**. When they do not fit together, one model does everything.

## Deleting a project
Choose **Delete project…** in the project menu. **Move to Trash** moves the whole folder (drafts, documents, attachments and house style); you can put it back from there. **Only remove from the list** leaves the folder where it is, for a folder you opened from elsewhere. Then the next project opens, or a new "My project".
