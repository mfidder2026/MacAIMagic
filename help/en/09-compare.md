**Compare** measures every downloaded model the same way and puts the results side by side: writing speed, prompt reading speed and memory use.

- Start a measurement; the app loads each model in turn.
- **Export measurement**: saves the results as a file. You choose where it goes.
- **Share on GitHub**: opens a form to share your measurement with other users. Nothing is sent automatically.

> Measure with as few other programs open as possible: they take memory and GPU time.
