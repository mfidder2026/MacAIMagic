## Asking a question
Type your question at the bottom and press **↩** or **⌘↩**. **⌥↩** starts a new line. The answer appears while the model writes; **Stop** ends it.

## Buttons below the input field
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Image | Add an image (or drag it in, or paste it with ⌘V). Works when *vision* is on. |
| Think | The model thinks first. Better for hard questions, but slower. Its thinking can be folded away. |
| Web | The model may search the web and read pages. |
| Work folder | Choose a folder the model may work in. |
| R W X | The rights in that folder: read, write, execute. |

## Context and summaries
A meter at the top of the conversation shows how much of the context it uses. At 80% the model summarizes the start; the messages stay visible, with a line where the summary begins. You can also have it done right away.

## When the model stops before it is done
Local models sometimes stop halfway. The app notices this, tells the model to go on, and shows that in the conversation. If it stops three times in a row, the app asks you to say "go on" or to give a hint.

## Answer settings
Under **Settings → Chat**: reasoning effort, how long the model may think (16,000 tokens by default), temperature (higher is more creative) and your own system prompt.

> Put general notes the model should know in every conversation in MACAIMAGIC.md: Settings → Chat → **Open file**.

Conversations are kept on this Mac. Delete one with a right-click in the sidebar.
